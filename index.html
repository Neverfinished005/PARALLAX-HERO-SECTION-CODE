The story of

Plot

Facing foreclosure of their homes in the Goon Docks area of Astoria, Oregon to an expanding country club, a group of children who call themselves "the Goonies" gather for a final weekend together.

Leader

Mikey

More Info
×

Michael "Mikey" Walsh

"Goonies never say die!"

When Mikey was younger, his father told him various stories of grand adventure, including the legend of One-Eyed Willy. Determined to save his family home from foreclosure, Mikey leads his friends deep underground into treacherous pirate caverns.

Heart & Soul

Chunk

More Info
×

Lawrence "Chunk" Cohen

"First you gotta do the Truffle Shuffle!"

Chunk is the only member of the Goonies to witness the police chase with the Fratellis. After being captured by the criminals, he befriends the gentle giant Sloth over a Baby Ruth candy bar, forming an unforgettable heroic duo.

Gadgeteer

Data

More Info
×

Richard "Data" Wang

"Pinchers of Power! You saved my life!"

A brilliant young inventor whose custom gadgets—including slick shoes, boxing-glove punchers, and suction cups—repeatedly rescue the Goonies from deadly pirate booby traps and the chasing Fratelli mobsters.

Linguist

Mouth

More Info
×

Clark "Mouth" Devereaux

"Yeah, but you know what? At least you're not in the dark."

Fast-talking, witty, and sarcastic, Mouth's fluency in Spanish allows him to translate the enigmatic poetry and musical notes inscribed upon One-Eyed Willy's map and copper bone skeleton keys.
